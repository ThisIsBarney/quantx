quantx_engine.contrib package¶

Experimental modules.

Their API may change, and the engine’s checks do not cover them. Most are pure Python and do not use the native engine: VQE and quantum chemistry, error correction and decoders, noise models and mitigation, quantum walks, bosonic codes, an IBM Quantum connector, a PennyLane device and more. A few are older interfaces to parts of the engine (lindblad, cached_dmrg). They moved here from quantx_engine in 0.2.0; the old paths warn, and go in 0.4.0. They are frozen: new features go to the engine API.

Import a module directly:

from quantx_engine.contrib.qec import SurfaceCode
