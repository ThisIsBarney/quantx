quantx_engine.models¶

Lattice models for the native symmetric (block-sparse) engine.

Build a model, then hand it to a solver:

from quantx_engine.models import HubbardChain, XXZChain
from quantx_engine.dmrg import ground_state

gs = ground_state(HubbardChain(L=16, U=4.0), n_up=8, n_dn=8, max_bond=512)
gs.energy

gs = ground_state(XXZChain(L=21, delta=0.5), sz=0.5)

The chains are open; HubbardLattice adds ladders, strips and cylinders (open along x, optionally periodic around y). The engine conserves the model’s U(1) charges exactly, so a solver works inside one symmetry sector: total S^z for the spin chains, the numbers of up and down electrons for Hubbard.

Conventions¶

Spin models use physical spin operators throughout, S = sigma/2 for spin-1/2 and the spin-1 matrices for spin-1, so couplings and energies compare directly with the literature (the open spin-1/2 Heisenberg chain has E/L -> 1/4 - ln 2 per site).

This differs from DMRGSolver(hamiltonian_type="heisenberg"), which keeps the engine’s Pauli convention, J sigma.sigma = 4 J S.S. The conversion happens here, in each model’s problem(): the engine’s spin-1/2 builders take Pauli couplings, so J is sent as J/4.

Classes

HubbardChain(L[, t, U, mu])

Fermi-Hubbard chain, H = -t sum_{i,s} (c+_{i s} c_{i+1 s} + h.c.) + U sum_i n_{i up} n_{i dn} - mu sum_i n_i.

HubbardLattice(Lx[, W, t, U, mu, y_periodic])

The Hubbard Hamiltonian of HubbardChain on a width-W lattice of Lx columns, open along x: W = 1 is the chain, W = 2 the two-leg ladder, and W >= 3 a strip, or a cylinder with y_periodic.

J1J2Chain(L[, J1, J2, delta])

Frustrated spin-1/2 chain, H = sum_i (J1 h_{i,i+1} + J2 h_{i,i+2}), with h_{ij} = Sx_i Sx_j + Sy_i Sy_j + delta Sz_i Sz_j.

Spin1Chain(L[, J])

Spin-1 Heisenberg chain, H = J sum_i S_i .

XXZChain(L[, J, delta])

Spin-1/2 XXZ chain, H = J sum_i (Sx_i Sx_{i+1} + Sy_i Sy_{i+1} + delta Sz_i Sz_{i+1}).

Exceptions

ModelError

Raised for an invalid model or symmetry sector.

Bases: _Hubbard

The Hubbard Hamiltonian of HubbardChain on a width-W lattice of Lx columns, open along x: W = 1 is the chain, W = 2 the two-leg ladder, and W >= 3 a strip, or a cylinder with y_periodic.

Sites are numbered column by column, site = W*x + y (see site()), and the engine runs them as one chain, so a hop along x has range W. Hopping is isotropic (t on every bond).
