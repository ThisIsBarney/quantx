quantx_engine.dyndecouple¶

M126: Quantum memory with dynamical decoupling.

Dynamical decoupling (DD) suppresses decoherence by applying periodic pulse sequences that average out system–bath interactions. This module provides:

  • DynamicalDecoupling — library of DD sequences (FID, Hahn, CPMG, XY4, XY8, KDD) and their filter functions.

  • QuantumMemory — single-qubit or multi-qubit quantum memory with configurable noise models and DD pulse sequences; simulates fidelity decay and coherence time enhancement.

DD Sequences¶

  • FID (free induction decay): no pulses, reference baseline.

  • Hahn Echo: single X pulse at t/2; cancels static inhomogeneity.

  • CPMG: n equally spaced X pulses; cancels slowly varying fields.

  • XY4: π-pulses in alternating X–Y pattern; first-order robust.

  • XY8: eight-pulse version of XY4; second-order robust.

  • KDD: Knill dynamical decoupling; robust to pulse imperfections.

Noise Models¶

  • Dephasing (T₂*): |0⟩⟨0| + e^{-t/T2*}|1⟩⟨1| off-diagonal decay.

  • Relaxation (T₁): amplitude damping.

  • Combined T₁/T₂ noise.

References

  • Viola, L. & Lloyd, S. (1998). Dynamical suppression of decoherence. Phys. Rev. A 58, 2733.

  • Gullion, T. et al. (1990). New, compensated Carr-Purcell sequences. J. Magn. Reson. 89, 479–484.

  • Souza, A. M. et al. (2011). Robust dynamical decoupling for quantum computing and quantum memory. Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 240501.

Physical validation¶

Hahn echo (CPMG-1): two pi pulses at t/4 and 3t/4 cancel quasi-static dephasing to first order. Filter function F(omega) = |1 - 2e^{i omega t/2} + 2e^{i omega 3t/4} - e^{i omega t}|^2 / omega^2. Low-frequency cutoff ~1/t. CPMG-n improves coherence time T2 by factor sqrt(n) for 1/f noise [Viola1999].

class quantx_engine.dyndecouple.QuantumMemory(T1=100.0, T2=50.0, pulse_error=0.0)[source]¶

Bases: object

Single-qubit quantum memory with configurable noise and DD sequences.

Simulates the time evolution of a qubit under: - Dephasing (T₂ decay of off-diagonal density matrix elements). - Amplitude damping (T₁ decay of excited state population). - DD pulse sequences that partially suppress decoherence.

Parameters:

  • T1 (float) – Longitudinal relaxation time (energy relaxation).

  • T2 (float) – Transverse dephasing time (T₂ ≤ 2T₁ for physical consistency).

  • pulse_error (float) – Probability of a π-pulse being imperfect (depolarising error per pulse).
