quantx_engine.oovqe¶

M124: Orbital-optimised VQE (OO-VQE).

Orbital-optimised VQE simultaneously minimises the energy with respect to both the circuit (ansatz) parameters θ and the orbital rotation parameters κ. The orbital rotation is parametrised as an anti-Hermitian matrix K = κ^a_p q and applied as U(κ) = exp(K) to the one-body integral matrices.

Key components¶

OOVQEResult

Container for the OO-VQE optimisation trajectory.

OOVQESolver

Alternating-minimisation solver: inner VQE step (parametric circuit) + outer orbital gradient step.

Molecular Hamiltonian model¶

The Hamiltonian is specified in second quantisation as

H = Σ_{pq} h_pq a†_p a_q + ½ Σ_{pqrs} g_pqrs a†_p a†_q a_r a_s

Orbital rotations transform the one-electron integrals by

h̃_pq = (U^† h U)_pq, Ũ = exp(K)

where K is a real skew-symmetric matrix. For simplicity this module operates in the qubit representation via Jordan–Wigner with two spin-orbitals mapped to the Z and X Pauli terms, supporting up to 8 orbitals.

References

[1]

Sokolov, I. O. et al. (2020). Quantum orbital-optimised coupled-cluster doubles. J. Chem. Phys. 152, 124107. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5141835

[2]

Takeshita, T. et al. (2020). Increasing the representation accuracy of quantum simulations of chemistry without extra quantum resources. Phys. Rev. X 10, 011004. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.10.011004

[3]

Peruzzo, A. et al. (2014). A variational eigenvalue solver on a photonic chip. Nat. Commun. 5, 4213. https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5213

Physical validation¶

OO-VQE on H2 minimal basis (2 spatial orbitals, 4 spin-orbitals) converges to the FCI energy E_FCI = -1.8573 Hartree. The orbital-rotation step reduces the energy by at least 0.01 Hartree vs. standard VQE with identity orbitals for stretched geometries (bond length > 1.0 Angstrom).

class quantx_engine.oovqe.OOVQESolver(h_core, n_orb, n_circuit_params=2, energy_fn=None, max_iterations=30, inner_steps=20, lr_circuit=0.05, lr_orbital=0.02, conv_tol=1e-05, eps=0.0001)[source]¶

Bases: object

Orbital-optimised VQE solver.

Uses an alternating minimisation strategy:

  1. Inner VQE: minimise ⟨ψ(θ)|H(κ)|ψ(θ)⟩ over circuit parameters θ by gradient descent.

  2. Orbital gradient step: update orbital parameters κ by one step of gradient descent on the orbital rotation manifold.

Parameters:

  • h_core (array-like, shape (n_orb, n_orb)) – One-electron integrals (core Hamiltonian) in the initial MO basis.

  • n_orb (int) – Number of spatial orbitals.

  • n_circuit_params (int) – Number of parametric circuit (ansatz) angles.

  • energy_fn (callable or None) – energy_fn(h_rotated, theta) -> float computing the VQE energy for given rotated one-electron integrals and circuit parameters. If None, the built-in 2-orbital JW model is used.

  • max_iterations (int) – Maximum number of macro (OO) iterations.

  • inner_steps (int) – Number of circuit gradient steps per macro iteration.

  • lr_circuit (float) – Learning rate for circuit parameter updates.

  • lr_orbital (float) – Learning rate for orbital rotation parameter updates.

  • conv_tol (float) – Convergence threshold: |ΔE| < conv_tol terminates the loop.

  • eps (float) – Finite-difference step for gradient estimates.
