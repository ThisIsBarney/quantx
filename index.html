QuantX¶

QuantX is a tensor-network engine for quantum many-body physics. It runs block-sparse MPS algorithms (DMRG, TDVP and fermionic HVA circuits) that conserve U(1), U(1)×U(1) and SU(2) charges exactly. The core is C++20, with a Python API.

These pages document what the engine does from Python and how each capability is checked. Everything else in the package is marked experimental.

What is verified, and against what¶

Capability

Python

Checked against

Ground states of the XXZ, J1-J2, spin-1 Heisenberg and Fermi-Hubbard chains, in a chosen symmetry sector

ground_state() with the models of quantx_engine.models

exact diagonalisation, written independently of the engine, to 1e-10

Hubbard ground states on ladders, strips and cylinders

ground_state(HubbardLattice(...))

exact diagonalisation on a ladder and a cylinder, where the fermion signs are physical, to 1e-10

Fermionic HVA (VQE) circuits on chains, ladders, strips and cylinders: energy, entanglement entropy, truncation

circuit(), layout()

an independent state vector: energy and entropies to 1e-10 from a product start; to 1e-6 from the default U = 0 start (about 1e-8 measured)

The gradient of an HVA circuit’s energy in every angle, by the adjoint method: one pass back through the circuit, with no circuit per angle

hva.circuit(..., gradient=True)

the exact gradient of the same independent state vector, to 1e-10 with svd="direct" (1e-6 on the default, faster route, whose rounding puts it a few 1e-9 off), and central differences of the engine’s own energy, to 1e-8; from a product start

Finite-temperature statics, the dynamical structure factor S(k, ω, T) and the optical conductivity σ(ω, T) of the XXZ, spin-1 and Hubbard chains

quantx_engine.thermal

exact diagonalisation of the whole Hilbert space, to the second-order Trotter error of the steps: below 1e-3 at the default step 0.05, falling by 4 at half the step; the transforms against Lehmann sums to 1e-8

These checks run in CI on a Linux build of the engine (test_models_ed.py, test_hva_statevector.py, test_hva_gradient.py and test_thermal_ed.py in python/quantx_engine/tests/).
